# NBA portrait licensing and delivery brief

Ball Knowledge requires one square player portrait for each of the 3,819 unique
players in its historical and active NBA roster. The app is a worldwide iOS App
Store product and stores assets in the app bundle for offline use.

The executed agreement must explicitly grant:

- worldwide mobile-app/App Store distribution for the intended release term;
- offline bundling and caching on user devices;
- cropping, resizing, and conversion to a square 512×512 app asset;
- use for historical and active NBA players; and
- sublicensing only as needed for Apple App Store distribution.

Please state every required credit, attribution placement, territory exclusion,
term, renewal/retention rule, and takedown obligation. Supply a written license
record and a small sample delivery before the complete delivery is accepted.

For each asset, provide a stable vendor player ID, a unique asset ID, filename,
source/delivery reference, creator and required credit (if any), full name,
birth date, and career context. Do not rely on player name as a unique key.

The supplied delivery is imported only after each vendor player ID has an
approved, evidence-backed mapping to the app roster's stable `playerID`.
Unmapped or ambiguous identities are returned in a manual-review queue. The
release gate requires 3,819/3,819 approved records and does not accept the
existing Basketball Reference images, which remain marked as source permission
required.
